About

From the classroom to credit models.

I studied mathematics education in Medan and spent several years teaching mathematics, in Indonesian and in English. Teaching taught me to explain a hard idea in more than one way until it lands.

I then completed a master's in mathematics with a data science specialization at Universitas Indonesia. Today I work at Bank Indonesia Institute, using NLP to study how clearly the central bank communicates. Before that, I spent six months at BRI building an LLM-based resume parser.

In my own projects I focus on credit risk, fraud and LLM tools for finance. I start with a simple baseline, split data by time, and write down release criteria before training, so that anyone who opens the repository can check the results.
